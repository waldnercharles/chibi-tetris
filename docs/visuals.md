# Stillwater in 3D

The upright well, beveled blocks, ghost outlines, hold, queue and text are mesh geometry. A translucent blue backing is strongest near the center and fades smoothly toward its edges. The inset grid and restrained rails keep the board readable. This material blends over the particle field; it does not sample or blur the scene behind it. Directional lighting, shadows, ambient occlusion, local event lights and bloom give the scene depth. Slow camera drift adds parallax without moving the logical grid. The opening pairs a real autoplayer board with music, mode and accessibility choices. Explicit Play releases every occupied demonstration row through the ordinary clear effect, eases the camera toward the normal view and fades in the gameplay HUD. Demo scoring never advances player progress. Reduced motion uses a brief stationary release and a direct camera change.

## Particle space

Four formations share stable particle identities: a turbulent nebula, a tilted accretion disk with a dark core, cellular blooms and stellar currents. A persisted launch counter changes the opening family; a seeded variation changes its composition. Ten-second quintic morphs respond to chapters, sustained changes in spectral balance, silence/resumption and longer musical phrases. They infer changes from sound rather than reading provider song metadata.

`content/shaders/CosmicVortex.glsl` and `CosmicStars.glsl` evaluate position, size, nonuniform scale, rotation, color and light response on the GPU. Their KDL descriptors expose typed parameters; shared GLSL helpers remain ordinary source files. This source path compiles directly and does not pass through the material graph's calculation nodes. Existing block and UI materials retain their graphs. Full density contains 131,072 main particles plus 16,384 distant grains. Reduced density uses 16,384 plus 2,048; effects off retains 512 stationary grains. Two instanced draws keep individual particles out of the CPU entity and behavior systems. Browser measurements, rather than the maximum count the engine supports, determine the shipped density.

A slow adaptive reference normalizes measured music while preserving quieter passages. A noise gate prevents room noise from becoming a full-strength visual. Bass shapes broad displacement, midrange bends flow, and treble affects fine structure and color. Measured onsets estimate tempo with a confidence score; silence clears that estimate. Four bounded world-space impulses carry player motion into the background as radial and tangential forces. Busy input adds to an existing nearby impulse without teleporting its origin. Stable hashes and smoothed envelopes avoid time-based star flicker. Projected attenuation keeps the single or Duet wells readable without an opaque wall. Pausing or reducing motion freezes particle travel and scene morphs. Stable per-particle identities blend between broad clouds, thin grains and stretched currents. Low-frequency density variation replaces star blinking; bloom is restrained so larger particles retain their facets. The GPU evaluates deterministic drag, gravity and prescribed curved flow. This is analytic particle motion, not a persistent fluid solver or collision simulation.

Three stage point lights, including one shadowed key, complement the directional light. Particles receive the direct and hemisphere lights at their vertices; they do not receive shadow maps or screen-space occlusion. No surrounding solid facets remain. A single softly beveled block shape has four restrained finishes with different roughness, metallic response and depth. An opaque graphite core writes depth beneath each colored coat, keeping the stack solid while its opacity and finish respond to the music. Materials and depth blend continuously during scene changes. The camera near plane preserves depth separation between the core and coat; ordinary blocks have low emission and bloom begins above the brighter event highlights. Music gives the four active blocks a small depth lift and elastic scale, without changing their logical cells, ghost, drop speed or lock timing.

## Action feedback

A reaction records all four occupied cell centers and rendered front-face depth before the next piece spawns. Motion, turn, hold and rejection cues begin at the physical edges. A hard drop sends four compact packets to the landing, followed by compression, scattered flecks and a short local light. Cleared rows emit from their original positions: a fracture front, tumbling lit chunks, thousands of fine embers and an expanding energy front. Each row emits 2,656 instances at full density, giving a four-row clear 10,624 instances. No full-height drop ribbons remain.

[The VFX layer sheet](vfx.md) specifies each layer's purpose, procedural material, emission shape, velocity, lifetime and blending, including the exact logarithmic birth distribution and smooth retirement curves. The fixed pool never overwrites a visible effect; saturation increments a diagnostic. GPU grains create no individual entities.

V cycles density. F toggles reduced motion: background travel, camera drift and audio modulation stop, while brief stationary input confirmations remain. G toggles the ghost. Compact opaque charcoal cards group audio, game mode and optional settings beside the attract board, with a white Play action and neutral unlit lettering. The 3D pause panel offers controls and a way to reconnect music; changed settings show their values immediately. Numbers reuse digit entities. Labels use filled, shallow extrusions of Geist, generated by the build-time C tool `tools/glyphs.c`. In-play text is limited to the board and its counters; detailed controls and audio guidance appear during music selection or pause.
