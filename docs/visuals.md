The upright well, beveled blocks, ghost outlines, hold, queue and text are mesh geometry. A translucent blue backing is strongest near the center and fades smoothly toward its edges. The inset grid and restrained rails keep the board readable. This material blends over the particle field; it does not sample or blur the scene behind it. Directional lighting, shadows, ambient occlusion, local event lights and bloom give the scene depth. Slow camera drift adds parallax without moving the logical grid. ## Particle space

The main particle field retains position, velocity and age on the GPU. Four force regimes produce clustered swarms, concentrated filaments, nested turbulent volumes and drifting dust. They replace the former accretion ring, outlined cells and parallel ribbons. A persisted launch counter varies the opening regime. Chapters, sustained spectral changes, silence/resumption and musical phrases change the forces over ten seconds; particles keep their existing momentum through the transition. Song changes are inferred from sound rather than provider metadata.

`content/shaders/CosmicVortex.glsl` owns the `advect` update and the `vortex` appearance entrypoint. Its KDL descriptor names both. The engine runs the update once per grain into two floating-point state textures, then the instanced mesh pass reads the current state. `CosmicStars.glsl` supplies the sparse distant layer. Both use ordinary GLSL and typed KDL parameters. Existing block and UI materials retain their graphs. Full density remains 131,072 main particles plus 16,384 distant grains; reduced density uses 16,384 plus 2,048. Effects off retains 512 stationary distant grains. Individual particles create no CPU entities or behaviors.

The adaptive music reference and noise gate preserve quieter passages. Bass and onsets accelerate the interior outward, midrange changes turbulence, and treble emphasizes sparse fine highlights. Four bounded world-space impulses carry player actions into the field as acceleration. Nearby inputs strengthen an existing impulse without changing its origin. Birth and retirement envelopes continuously fade alpha and size. Most grains are tiny, nearly isotropic solids. Only rare fast foreground grains stretch along their velocity; depth attenuation separates the cloud's layers. Projected attenuation keeps the single or Duet wells quiet. Pausing and reduced motion freeze state and the musical controls.

The GPU integrates persistent particle motion through multiscale curl fields, exponential velocity relaxation and soft confinement. This is particle advection, with no pressure solve, mutual particle collisions or gravity solver. [The reference study](reference-particle-flow.md) records the concrete differences from Particula and the Tetris Spheres film.

Three stage point lights, including one shadowed key, complement the directional light. Particles receive the direct and hemisphere lights at their vertices; they do not receive shadow maps or screen-space occlusion. No surrounding solid facets remain. A single softly beveled block shape has four restrained finishes with different roughness, metallic response and depth. An opaque graphite core writes depth beneath each colored coat, keeping the stack solid while its opacity and finish respond to the music. Materials and depth blend continuously during scene changes. The camera near plane preserves depth separation between the core and coat; ordinary blocks have low emission and bloom begins above the brighter event highlights. Music gives the four active blocks a small depth lift and elastic scale, without changing their logical cells, ghost, drop speed or lock timing.

## Action feedback

A reaction records all four occupied cell centers and rendered front-face depth before the next piece spawns. Motion, turn, hold and rejection cues begin at exposed physical edges. A hard drop confirms contact immediately with compression, scattered flecks and a short local light; a brief granular wash contracts through the traveled space. Cleared rows emit from their original positions: a fracture front, tumbling lit chunks, thousands of fine embers and an expanding energy front. Each row emits 2,656 instances at full density, giving a four-row clear 10,624 instances. No full-height drop ribbons remain.

A final loss detaches the existing colored blocks into an accelerating upward tumble. Hold, Next and the board stay fixed during departure; results wait until the release has developed. The scoring grid remains intact, and reconnecting audio cannot restore the retired meshes.

[The VFX layer sheet](vfx.md) specifies each layer's purpose, procedural material, emission shape, velocity, lifetime and blending, including the exact logarithmic birth distribution and smooth retirement curves. The fixed pool never overwrites a visible effect; saturation increments a diagnostic. GPU grains create no individual entities.

V cycles density. F toggles reduced motion: background travel, camera drift and audio modulation stop, while brief stationary input confirmations remain. G toggles the ghost. Compact opaque charcoal cards group audio, game mode and optional settings beside the attract board, with a white Play action and neutral unlit lettering. The 3D pause panel offers controls and a way to reconnect music; changed settings show their values immediately. Numbers reuse digit entities. Labels use filled, shallow extrusions of Geist, generated by the build-time C tool `tools/glyphs.c`. In-play text is limited to the board and its counters; detailed controls and audio guidance appear during music selection or pause.
